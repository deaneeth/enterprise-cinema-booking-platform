### Ethical Protocols Followed

1. **Informed Consent**: All participants were briefed about the purpose of the study before signing consent forms
2. **Voluntary Participation**: Participants were informed they could withdraw at any time without penalty
3. **Data Anonymization**: Personal identifiable information is stored separately from test results
4. **Secure Data Handling**: All data is handled following academic research standards
5. **Confidentiality**: Participant responses and feedback are kept confidential and only used for system improvement
6. **No Deception**: Participants were fully informed about the nature and purpose of the testing
