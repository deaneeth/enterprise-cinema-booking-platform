# UAT Post-Test Survey — Cinema Booking System (CineBook)

## Survey Form (Administered to Each Participant)

### Section A: Task-Specific Ratings

Rate 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree):

1. The booking process was easy to complete. `1  2  3  4  5`
2. Seat selection was clear and understandable. `1  2  3  4  5`
3. Checkout/payment steps were clear. `1  2  3  4  5`
4. I could find what I wanted (movie/showtime) easily. `1  2  3  4  5`
5. I would use this system in real life. `1  2  3  4  5`

### Section B: Open Questions

- What was the most confusing part?
- What would you improve first?
- Any bugs or unexpected behaviour you noticed?
- What did you like most about the system?
