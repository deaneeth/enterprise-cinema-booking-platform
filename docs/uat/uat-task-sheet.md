# UAT Task Sheet — Cinema Booking System (CineBook)

*This sheet is given to each participant at the start of their session*

---

## Welcome

Thank you for participating in our usability testing session!

**Important Notes**:
- Please think aloud while using the system — tell us what you're thinking as you navigate
- There are no right or wrong answers — if something is confusing, please say so!
- I (the moderator) cannot help unless you are fully stuck for more than 2 minutes
- We are testing the **system**, not you — any issues you encounter help us improve
- You can stop at any time if you feel uncomfortable

**Estimated Duration**: 25-35 minutes

---

## Task 1 — Create Account & Book Tickets (Core Flow)

**Goal**: Register as a new user and book 2 seats for a movie

**Steps**:
1. Navigate to the homepage
2. Click "Sign Up" or "Register" to create a new account
3. Fill in your details and complete registration
4. Browse the available movies
5. Pick a movie that interests you
6. Select a cinema and showtime
7. Select 2 seats from the seat map
8. Proceed to checkout
9. Complete payment using PayPal Sandbox (login: `sb-buyer@personal.example.com`)
10. View your booking confirmation
11. Navigate to "My Bookings" or booking history to verify your booking appears

**Success**: You have successfully booked 2 seats and can see the booking in your history

---

## Task 2 — Understand & Change Seat Selection

**Goal**: Understand the seat map and change your selected seats before paying

**Steps**:
1. Start a new booking (pick any movie/showtime)
2. On the seat map, observe the different seat colors
3. Select 2 seats
4. Now, deselect those seats (click them again)
5. Select 2 different seats in another row
6. Verify the price updates correctly
7. Continue to checkout (you may exit before paying)

**Success**: You can select and deselect seats freely, and understand what each color means

---

## Task 3 — Find a Specific Movie

**Goal**: Use search and/or filters to find a specific movie and showtime

**Steps**:
1. Go to the movies page or homepage
2. Use the search bar to search for "Avatar"
3. Use filters (if available) to narrow by genre or date
4. Find an evening showtime (after 6 PM) for the movie
5. Click on it to view details

**Success**: You found the correct movie and identified an evening showtime

---

## Task 4 — Cancel a Booking

**Goal**: Cancel an existing booking and verify the system updates

**Steps**:
1. Navigate to "My Bookings" or booking history
2. Find the booking you made in Task 1
3. Click to view booking details
4. Click "Cancel Booking"
5. Confirm the cancellation
6. Verify the booking status changes to "Cancelled"

**Success**: Your booking shows as cancelled in your history

---

## Task 5 — Write a Review (Optional)

**Goal**: Submit a review for a movie

**Steps**:
1. Navigate to any movie detail page
2. Scroll down to the reviews section
3. Click "Write a Review" or similar button
4. Give the movie a star rating (1-5)
5. Write a brief review (1-2 sentences is fine)
6. Submit your review
7. Verify your review appears on the page

**Success**: Your review is visible on the movie page

---

## Post-Task Questions

After completing the tasks, your moderator will ask you:

1. Rate from 1-5: How easy was the booking process?
2. Rate from 1-5: How clear was the seat selection?
3. Rate from 1-5: How clear was the checkout/payment?
4. Rate from 1-5: How easy was it to find movies?
5. Rate from 1-5: Would you use this system in real life?

**Open Questions**:
- What was the most confusing part?
- What would you improve first?
- Did you notice any bugs or unexpected behavior?
- What did you like most about the system?
